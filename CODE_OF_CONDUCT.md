# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project evaluates how models speak to people under stress. Participation
here follows the same rule: take the other person's actual situation seriously,
and do not use tone to assert status.

Expected behaviour:

- Argue with the case, not the person who filed it.
- Take disagreement seriously enough to write a counter-case.
- Treat "this is unclear, and here is why" as a real contribution.
- Say when a metric is weak instead of tuning the case to hide it.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Personal attacks, condescension, or deliberate stonewalling.
- Wholly dismissive replies ("this is obvious", "did you even read the RFC").
- Pressure to land a specific policy regardless of what the cases show.

## Scope

Applies in issues, PRs, RFC discussions, and any space where someone is
representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers privately. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers will acknowledge within a week, and will state the outcome and the
reasoning. Maintainers who do not follow this standard are subject to the same
process as anyone else.
